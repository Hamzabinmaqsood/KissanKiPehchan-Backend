Introduction

Land Information & Management System (LIMS) ("the Company," "we," "our" or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy. We will never share your personally identifiable information with third parties without your consent.

This policy describes the types of information we may collect from you or that you may provide when you:

  • Visit, access, or use our mobile applications ("apps") running on smartphones, tablets, and other devices;
  • Communicate with us electronically and through other channels;
  • View or click on our ads or online content; and
  • Interact with us through social media platforms and associated services.

Please read this policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, choose not to use our application. By accessing or using our app, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Legal Age

No one under the age of 18 may provide any personal information to or on our app without verified legal parental or guardian consent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from persons under 18. If we learn that we have received personal information from a minor without verification of parental consent, we will delete that information immediately. If you believe we might have any information regarding a minor without proper consent, please contact us at ceolimspakistan@gmail.com.

SMS Access & OTP Permissions Notice

Read SMS & Receive SMS Disclosure:

Our app requests explicit permission to read and receive SMS messages strictly to automatically detect and retrieve One-Time Passwords (OTP) sent by our system for user account registration, multi-factor login, and identity verification.

  • Scope: Access is limited exclusively to OTP auto-fill and security alerts sent by LIMS.
  • Privacy: We do NOT read, store, transmit, or process unrelated personal SMS messages from your inbox.
  • Data Retention: OTP data is processed temporarily in-memory and discarded instantly following successful authentication.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our services, we collect several types of data:

A. Account & Profile Data

  • Contact Details: Full name, mailing address, telephone number, email address, and unique account identifiers.
  • Financial & Payment History: Purchase history and service subscriptions. Note: We do not store raw credit/debit card numbers or mobile money (MoMo) financial credentials on our infrastructure.
  • Profile Metrics: Date of birth, gender, profession, agricultural income range, marital status, and optional social media profile identifiers (e.g., Facebook login avatar).
  • User Contributions: Publicly shared media, discussion forum posts, and audio notes.

B. Device and System Telemetry

  • Hardware Specs: Device make and model, operating system version, software build identifiers, and unique device hardware IDs.
  • Location Data: Real-time precise geographic position obtained via GPS, cellular tower signals, Wi-Fi, or Bluetooth (subject to device permissions).
  • Network Metrics: Mobile carrier name, IP address, connection speed, browser engine type, language, and system time zone.

How We Use Your Information

We process collected information to:

  • Deliver and optimize core agricultural advisory services, satellite map rendering, and report generation.
  • Process transactions, billing, and subscription management.
  • Authenticate user identity, protect against fraudulent access, and enforce system security.
  • Analyze system load, optimize server infrastructure, and speed up search queries.
  • Personalize app content based on your region, crop selections, and historical usage patterns.

Automated Tracking & Third-Party Integration

We utilize standard tracking tools including application cookies, pixel web beacons, and server log files to evaluate feature utilization and preserve session integrity.

Certain third-party service providers (such as analytical tools or embedded components) may collect anonymous device telemetry over time. We do not control third-party tracking mechanisms directly; you can manage cookie consent and location access directly inside your device's native OS privacy settings.

2. Security Procedures

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your authentication credentials. Do not disclose your security PINs, passwords, or device access to unauthorized third parties.

While we employ enterprise-grade encryption protocols and security best practices to protect your personal information, no internet transmission or electronic database storage is completely immune to breach. Absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

3. Availability of App

The application is provided on an "AS IS" and "AS AVAILABLE" basis. LIMS reserves the right to suspend or update services for system maintenance, infrastructure updates, or unexpected network carrier outages without prior liability.
