Introduction

To maintain a safe, respectful, and reliable community, all users of the Land Information & Management System (LIMS) application must observe the standards set out in this Community Usage Policy.

LIMS maintains a zero-tolerance policy for objectionable content and abusive users.

1. Respectful Conduct

  • Use respectful and professional language at all times. Foul, abusive, offensive, threatening, discriminatory, or inappropriate language or content is strictly prohibited.

2. Accuracy of Information

  • Ensure that all information, data, images, documents, uploads, and scans submitted through the application are accurate, genuine, relevant, and, to the best of the user's knowledge, complete.
  • Do not knowingly upload false, misleading, manipulated, fraudulent, or unauthorized information.

3. Privacy of Other Users

  • Respect the privacy and rights of other users and do not upload or share another person's personal information without appropriate authorization.

4. Lawful and Intended Use

  • Use the application's features and community platforms only for their intended and lawful purposes.
  • Do not misuse, disrupt, damage, attempt to gain unauthorized access to, or interfere with the application's systems, services, or other users.

5. Zero Tolerance & Enforcement

LIMS has zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users. Any content or behaviour that violates this policy may be reported from within the application.

Reported content is reviewed within 24 hours. Content found to be in violation of this policy is removed, and the user responsible is ejected from the service where appropriate.

Users may also block any other user. Once blocked, that user's content is removed from the blocking user's feed immediately.

LIMS reserves the right to review, remove, restrict, or take appropriate action regarding any content, information, or user activity that violates this Community Usage Policy, applicable laws, or other LIMS policies.

By using the application, you agree to comply with this Community Usage Policy.
